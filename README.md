# Memorizify: Productivity-Enhancing iOS Application

This repository contains the source code for my Diploma Thesis project, an iOS application called Memorizify. Visit [https://dspace.cvut.cz/handle/10467/114551](https://dspace.cvut.cz/handle/10467/114551) for full Thesis text and original attachments.

## About Memorizify

Memorizify is more than just a Pomodoro timer—it’s a powerful and engaging study companion designed to make learning both effective and enjoyable. At its core, it uses the proven Pomodoro technique to boost focus and productivity, but it takes things further by transforming routine study sessions into immersive experiences. Through captivating storylines that unfold as you study, Memorizify keeps users engaged and motivated, turning each session into a mini-adventure. This unique narrative-driven approach helps users stay focused for longer periods, making studying feel less like a chore and more like a journey.

What truly sets Memorizify apart is its emphasis on social learning. Users can form guilds with friends, creating a collaborative environment where they can study together, track each other’s progress, and participate in friendly competitions. This shared experience fosters accountability and motivation, as well as a sense of community. Whether you're working toward a group goal or simply supporting each other’s individual progress, studying becomes more rewarding when done together. It’s a fresh take on education that leverages the power of teamwork to enhance personal growth.

Beyond productivity and collaboration, Memorizify provides all the essential tools to keep your learning organized and engaging. With built-in progress tracking, interactive features, and personalized study journeys, it becomes your all-in-one academic ally. Whether you're prepping for exams, tackling long-term projects, or simply aiming to build better habits, Memorizify supports you every step of the way. It’s not just about studying harder—it’s about studying smarter, with a little more fun along the way.
